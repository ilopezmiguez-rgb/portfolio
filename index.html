ROMANA

Shared expense tracker that minimizes settlement friction

THE PROBLEM

Splitting shared costs is universally painful. Existing tools are either too complex for casual use or too primitive for real group dynamics: late joiners, unequal splits, multiple settlements over time. The gap Romana targets is precise — you just paid, your friends are still at the table, and you need to log it in three taps.

KEY PRODUCT DECISIONS
01
Settlements are immutable. A settlement is a fact — not an editable record. Allowing edits would corrupt balance history. The schema enforces this with a no-DELETE policy, because the right product decision and the right data decision are the same decision.
02
Store resolved amounts, not split types. Whether even, percentage, or by item — expense_shares always stores "person X owes $Y." Balance calculation becomes a simple SUM. New split types only need a new form — zero schema changes.
03
Seven extension points from day one. Multi-currency, recurring expenses, receipt photos — none require schema refactors. The architecture of V1 respects the roadmap of V3. Future phases are planned before the first line of code.

CRÉDITO CLARO

Transparent loan simulator built on a single hypothesis

Try the Live Demo →
THE HYPOTHESIS

Most Argentine credit simulators show a monthly payment and nothing else. Users don't understand how much interest they're paying, can't compare scenarios, and don't know what TNA means. The hypothesis: greater cost transparency increases conversion to loan applications.

KEY PRODUCT DECISIONS
01
Offline-first data strategy. INDEC inflation data was pre-fetched and cached statically instead of calling a live API. This eliminates a class of failure modes in demo contexts — a deliberate trade-off of real-time accuracy for reliability. The product works regardless of external dependencies.
02
French amortization as default. Most consumer credit in Argentina uses fixed installments. Matching the mental model users already carry from their real-world loans is a product decision, not just a technical one.
03
Explicit scope exclusion. Real scoring, personalized offers, and live rate feeds were deferred — documented, not forgotten. V1 validates one thing: does transparency itself move the needle on conversion?
WHAT I'D MEASURE
completion_rate % reaching full results after adjusting ≥1 parameter
comparator_usage signals active decision-making, not passive browsing
cta_click_rate the conversion event the transparency hypothesis is built around
